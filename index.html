Bio: Currently I am an undergraduate student in Zhejiang University, majoring in Information Engineering. I worked as a research intern at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems(MPI-IS), supervised by Prof. Andreas Geiger and Prof. Yiyi Liao. I minor in the 13th Morningside Cultural China Scholars Program(MCCSP) and Intensive Training Program of Innovation and Entrepreneurship (ITP), Chu Kochen Honors College. I am dedicated to enhance my professional skills in the field of machine learning and cultivate the ability of innovation and entrepreneurship.

Research interest: novel and efficient machine learning, including

  • Neural Implicit Rendering and Representation Learning
  • Efficient Neural Networks Design
  • Convex Optimization
